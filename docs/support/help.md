## When you open a ticket

Help us help you faster — include:

1. The **cluster** and **login node** you were using (e.g., Easley, Hopper).
2. The **job ID** (`squeue -u $USER`, or from `sacct`) for job problems.
3. The **exact command** you ran and the **complete error message**.
4. The path to your **batch script** and log files (we can read them with
   your permission).
